Our training welcomes everyone, whether you already use art in your work or are just beginning to explore it.

Over two days, you'll experience hands-on learning to expand your tools for healing, resilience, and connection. By attending this training you'll join a community of 1,500+ Windows Facilitators that includes advocates, teachers, social workers, case managers, therapists, community organizers, and more.



As a Certified Windows Facilitator, you'll walk away with:

  • Trauma-informed practices shaped by 30+ years of experience

  • 600+ ready-to-use, low barrier art workshops for all ages and settings

  • A research-informed trauma treatment model you can integrate into all your services
